Klee

A 2D generative composition toy — drag shapes, tap planes, hold to pulse. Every arrangement blooms into a balanced Bauhaus-style composition.

Palette & Shapes

Primary palette: red · yellow · blue · black · white

Core Verbs

Drag — place geometric primitives from toolbar onto the grid
Tap — fill an empty cell with a single-color plane
Hold — pulse a shape to grow, emit, and resonate
